Give your <DocTerm term="agent" lang="en" label="AI agent" /> access to your Telegram or MAX account. You ask in plain words — `who is
still waiting for my answer?`, `what did we agree with Marco in September?` — and the agent
reads your chats and answers, with the messages it used as proof.

It works through a command line tool (<DocTerm term="cli" lang="en" label="cli" />) for your messenger: `tg` for Telegram, `max` for MAX. You
need only the one for the messenger you use. The tool runs on your computer and uses your own
account. Your agent (Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Gemini CLI, Hermes and <DocTerm term="supported-agents" lang="en" label="any other agent" />) runs it for you. You decide
what the agent may do: only read, ask before sending, or send to chosen chats.

Your messages stay between you and the messenger. The tool talks straight to Telegram or MAX, keeps
its archive on your computer, and sends nothing to WireCat: there is no WireCat service in between. Your agent
sees only the messages it reads for a task.

## What you can ask

**Catch up**

```text prompt compact
Summarise unread messages and tell me who needs an answer.
```

**Find**

```text prompt compact
Find the price we agreed in Atlas and show the message.
```

**Prepare**

```text prompt compact
Prepare an agenda for my call with Anna from this week’s chats.
```

**Follow up**

```text prompt compact
What did I promise this week? Draft replies, don’t send them.
```

**Personal messages to many people**

```text prompt compact
Draft a personal reply to each person who asked about the workshop.
```

## What it covers

- **Your whole account** — messages, chats, groups, contacts, polls: what you do in the app.
- **Your history, searchable offline** — a local archive of the chats you choose.
- **Every Bot API method** — all 185 for Telegram, all 33 for MAX.
- **Group administration** — review activity, manage members and moderate groups where you have admin rights.
- **Voice messages to text** — on your own computer.
- **Limits you set** — read-only, ask before sending, chosen chats only.

## Start here

1. **[Install and log in](./installation.mdx)** — about 5 minutes. You install the CLI and scan
   a QR code with your phone.
2. **[Try your first tasks](./first-tasks.md)** — copy a request into your agent and get your
   first answer.

The installed <DocTerm term="skill" lang="en" label="skill" /> gives your agent instructions for using the messenger. Your agent’s own settings determine where the messages it reads are processed.

That is all you need to start. Later, when you need them:

- [Writing requests](./prompting.mdx) — get better answers from your agent.
- [Connect your AI assistant](./agents.mdx) — if your agent does not see the `tg` or `max` command.
- <DocTerm term="mcp" lang="en" label="MCP" />: [MCP and skills](./mcp.mdx) — for Claude Desktop and other clients without a terminal.
- ChatGPT, Claude or another AI chat in a browser or on your phone: [Use from web or mobile](./browser-apps.mdx) — your own tunnel and a one-time login code.
