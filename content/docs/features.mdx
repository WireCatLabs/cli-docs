Ask your AI agent about your own work and get an answer you can check. "What did we agree with Acme?", "Who is waiting for my reply?", "What was decided on Tuesday's call?" The agent answers from your chats, mail, calls and documents, and shows the message or passage behind each part of the answer.

You connect the sources you use, one tool for each, and keep the agent you already like: Claude Code, Codex, ChatGPT or another. Everything the tools copy stays on your computer, in one local store. Memo, the notes tool, searches your messages, mail, notes, meetings and deals there at once. Start with one tool, for the source you use most: [Installation](./installation.mdx).

What you can do:

- **Messages with context:** find what was said, what changed and who needs an answer.
- **Search with evidence:** get the matching message and what was said around it.
- **Meetings, calls and deals:** recall a decision from a call or check where a deal stands.
- **People across accounts:** keep the accounts of one person together, once you confirm they match.
- **Notes, files and tasks:** bring chosen folders, your own notes and your to-dos into the same search.
- **Bots and groups:** answer through a bot or help run a community.
- **Control over access:** choose the sources and what the tools may change or send.

## Connect the agent you use

WireCat supplies tools and access to your sources. Your AI agent interprets your request and uses those tools to gather information or perform the actions you permit. You can change agents without starting your message archive or notes again.

Use a local agent with terminal access, or connect a compatible app through MCP. Available capabilities depend on the connected tool and account.

[Connect an AI agent](./agents.mdx) · [Use from web or mobile](./browser-apps.mdx)

## Messages

Catch up on unread messages, find questions awaiting an answer and prepare for a conversation using earlier messages. Ask the agent to show the source messages behind a summary or proposed reply, so you can check an agreement before acting on it.

Your agent can save a reply as a draft in a Telegram chat or in the Drafts folder of your mailbox. You open it, check the text and send it yourself. In MAX, WhatsApp and Slack the agent returns the text in your agent chat, and you paste it into the chat. Sending is a separate action with its own permissions.

[Try a first task](./first-tasks.mdx) · [Drafts and templates](./drafts-and-templates.mdx)

## Search and sources

Find an older message by words, person, chat or date, or look for a topic by meaning when semantic search is configured. Open the surrounding messages to understand a result. Search can combine stored messages, imported notes and mail through Memo.

The local archive contains the history you downloaded. An empty result does not prove that a message never existed: check the period covered and import results. Word search can work locally without an AI provider; search by meaning needs additional setup.

[Find messages](./search.mdx) · [Try the search playground](./search.mdx#try-it-with-sample-messages) · [Search by meaning](./tg/topic-search.md)

## People across accounts

Keep context about a person together across the accounts you explicitly link. Use it to recall what you discussed, who introduced you or which notes you saved about them. Matching names alone do not merge people.

The answer depends on stored history and confirmed links; ask for evidence when the agent draws a conclusion about someone.

[People and linked accounts](./people.md)

## Meetings and calls

Find what was decided on a call and who took which task, next to the chats and mail on the same subject. Zoom meetings, Google Meet calls from your calendar and calls from your phone system are kept on your computer as meetings, with their transcripts. A phone call recording is turned into text on your computer, unless you choose a cloud speech service for it.

[Zoom meetings](./zm/meetings.md) · [Google Meet](./google-meet.mdx) · [Bitrix24 calls](./b24/calls.md) · [Asterisk](./pbx/index.md)

## Clients and deals

Ask where a deal stands, who is responsible for it and what the client was told last. The CRM tools copy your deals, contacts and companies from Bitrix24, amoCRM, HubSpot or Attio into the same local store, so the agent sees them beside your chats and calls. When you ask for a change, such as moving a deal to the next stage, it goes to the CRM itself.

[Bitrix24](./b24/index.md) · [amoCRM and Kommo](./amo/index.md) · [HubSpot](./hubspot/index.md) · [Attio](./attio/index.md)

## Notes, files and email

Use Memo to import selected folders, including Obsidian notes, search readable file contents and keep notes linked to people or messages. Imported files retain source references; notes you create can be edited and exported. Tags help you collect related sources without changing the originals.

The mail tool, `mail`, copies your mail into the same local store, and Memo searches it next to your notes and messages. Memo reads the messenger history other tools already downloaded; it does not connect to a messenger itself. Some document formats need optional readers, and scanned pages need text recognition first.

[Notes and files](./memo/index.md) · [Email](./mail/email.md)

## Tasks and memory

Keep the questions you have not answered and the promises others made you as tasks, so they do not get lost in your chats. Ask for today's agenda, turn a message into a task and close it when it is done. The agent can also remember what it learned, such as a preference or a decision, with the sources that show it, and you choose what is kept.

[Tasks](./tasks.md) · [Memories and facts](./memo/memories.md)

## Bots

Use a bot's own identity for messages, button responses and community workflows. An agent can help operate the bot through the supported Bot API commands. A bot's access differs from your personal account: it cannot read your private chats or assume your account's history.

Auto-reply rules and templates can handle selected incoming messages while their process is running, within the audience and limits you configure.

[Bot setup and capabilities](./bot-api.mdx) · [Auto-reply templates](./drafts-and-templates.mdx)

## Groups and channels

Ask your agent to review unanswered questions, help with members and invitations, or apply moderation rules. Changes require the account's administrator rights and tool permissions. Ongoing moderation needs a running process or a schedule; installing a tool does not start it automatically.

[Group administration](./group-analytics.mdx#group-admins) · [Telegram groups](./tg/groups.md) · [MAX groups](./max/groups.md)

## Your data and permissions

The tools keep history and notes on the computer or server where you run them. Local storage lets you search saved data without repeatedly downloading it. Background updates need that machine and the relevant process to stay running.

Choose the accounts, history and folders you make available, and set permissions for changes and sending. Local storage does not mean an agent's AI provider receives no data: retrieved content can enter the agent's request. CLI controls also do not constrain unrelated access an agent has to your machine.

[Where to install](./installation.mdx) · [Permissions](./configuration.mdx#permissions) · [Security and data flow](./security.mdx)
