The **Clients** page in WireCat Desktop shows the deals and leads from your CRM, today Bitrix24, as a table or as a
board by stage. Use it to see where each deal stands and to move a deal to its next stage without opening the CRM. A
move here goes to Bitrix24 too. Your agents read the same deals, so they can answer "what is open with Acme?" from the
same picture. The page needs Memo, and moving a card also needs the Bitrix24 tool.

Clients is marked beta: you see a β beside its name. The section is new and lightly tested.

## Before you start

1. On the Tools page, install Bitrix24 and sign in. Sign-in asks for a webhook address from your portal.
2. Choose whose deals are copied. A sync copies only the deals and leads of the CRM users you choose, and the clients
   on them, never the whole portal. Until you choose, a sync copies nothing and says how.
   [Whose deals are copied](../people.md#whose-deals-are-copied) shows the commands.
3. On the Clients page, click **Import from Bitrix24**. With more than one Bitrix24 profile, **Import from a CRM**
   asks which.

The line **Sync** under the buttons names the CRM account and **Chosen users**. Its ⓘ repeats what is copied. If an
account is set to copy everyone's deals, the page says that its sync stops and offers **Only mine**.

## Table or board

**View** switches between **Table** and **Board**, and **Deals** and **Leads** switch what you see. The search field
and the filters for account, pipeline, responsible person and stage work in both views.

- **Table:** **Title**, **Stage**, **Amount**, **Pipeline**, **Contact** and **Updated**, with a pager below.
- **Board:** one column per stage. A long column shows its first cards and pages the rest. The **Unsorted** column
  holds cards that have no stage yet.

## Move a deal to another stage

Do it in one of three places:

- On the board, drag the card to another column. With the keyboard, select the card, press Space, move it with the
  arrow keys and press Space again; Escape cancels.
- In the table, choose a stage in the row's **Stage** list.
- Open the deal and choose its stage in the card.

If one column holds two stages of the deal's pipeline, a dialog asks which one. The move is sent to Bitrix24. Without
the Bitrix24 tool, or with a version too old to move deals, the page is **Read only**, and the chip says what to
install or update.

## Map stages

Every CRM names its stages its own way. **Map stages** puts each CRM stage under one of your stages, so deals from
every CRM line up on one board. The dialog opens by itself the first time a CRM brings stages you have not mapped yet.

- Each CRM stage that waits sits in the **Unmapped** column, with a **Suggested** stage. Click the tick to accept the
  suggestion, choose another stage from its menu, or drag it to a column.
- **Accept suggestions** accepts every suggestion at once.
- You can add, rename, move and remove your own stages.

Until you confirm it, a deal stands under the suggested stage, marked **Not confirmed**.

## Notices

Notices fold into one line of chips above the board, such as "6 stages to confirm" or **Read only**. Click a chip to
read its notice. The stages chip has **Accept all**, which confirms every suggested stage, and **Confirm stages**,
which opens the dialog.
