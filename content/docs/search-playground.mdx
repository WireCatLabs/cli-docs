Try search before installing or logging in: choose a chat, date or attachment type and see which messages remain. Then open nearby messages to understand the context and check a conclusion.

We prepared a small training conversation for this example: 36 fictional messages from 6 Telegram and MAX chats. These are sample data for learning search; no account is needed. You will learn to narrow a query and check what supports an answer.

1. Pick a chat or message-type filter — for example, messages with links.
2. Open a match and its nearby context.
3. Change a filter and compare the results.
