Build a query against a fictional Telegram/MAX archive. Choose words, chats, people and dates, inspect surrounding messages, and check the sources. This playground stays on your device and never connects to an account.

Choose `has:` to try files, links, photos, video, audio, voice messages, stickers, contacts, locations or polls. The sample contains 36 messages in 6 chats; `has:attachment` includes attached media, while `has:link` finds links in the message text.
