## Try the conversation

You have a project call tomorrow. Try gathering context, checking other chats and preparing
an agenda. This is the same scenario as on the homepage: you send prepared requests and the
agent shows its commands and answers. The messages are fictional; no account connection is needed.

<MeetingGuide lang="en" />

## Get your meeting brief

To try it with your chats, copy a suitable request into your [agent](./agents.md) with a
[connected account](./installation.mdx). It will check the conversation and prepare a brief.
If history is missing, ask it to download the relevant period: [Telegram](./tg/archive.md)
or [MAX](./max/archive.md).

Check the source messages and the list of chats read. Then refine the request: for example,
“Also check my chat with the contractor” or “Keep only deadline questions in the agenda”.
[Refining your requests](./prompting.md).
