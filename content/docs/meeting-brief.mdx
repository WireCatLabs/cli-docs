## Try the conversation

Choose a scenario and press Send. These examples come from the homepage: the messages are
fictional, and no account connection is needed. The next follow-up appears after the agent answers.

<MeetingGuide lang="en" />

## Get your meeting brief

To try it with your chats, copy a suitable request into your [agent](./agents.mdx) with a
[connected account](./installation.mdx). It will check the conversation and prepare an answer.
If history is missing, ask it to download the relevant period: [Telegram](./tg/archive.md)
or [MAX](./max/archive.md).

Check the source messages and the list of chats read. Then refine the request: for example,
“Also check my chat with the contractor” or “Keep only deadline questions in the agenda”.
[Refining your requests](./prompting.mdx).
