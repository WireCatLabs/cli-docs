Never lose the transcripts of your Google Meet calls. They stay on your computer after Google removes them from Meet,
30 days after the call. You can always find the call you need and link it to its calendar event, your chats and your
notes, so your agent has the whole context of the meeting.

An agent with access to your calls tells you what was decided, who joined and who said what, and quotes the passage
with its time. It finds a call by its calendar event, by a person or by a word that was said.

There is no separate Meet tool: Google Calendar's tool `gcal` works with Google Meet. It keeps calls, participants and
transcripts, points to the call recordings and Gemini notes on Google Drive, makes rooms and adds a Meet link to an
event. You need `gcal` installed and signed in to your Google account ([installation](./gcal/installation.md)).
Transcripts come only from a Google Workspace account: a personal Google account gets calls and participants without
transcripts.

## Short version

The examples use the profile `personal`, the name you gave your Google account in `gcal`. Use yours.

```sh
gcal personal session start --grant meetings
gcal personal meetings sync
gcal personal meetings list started_at>=2026-11-01
gcal personal meetings transcript 12
```

The first command signs in again with read access to Google Meet. The sync keeps the calls of the last 30 days. The
list shows them newest first, and `meetings transcript` shows what was said in call 12, line by line.

A new sign-in replaces the permissions the profile had. If you also change events with `gcal`, ask for both at once:
`gcal personal session start --grant write,meetings`. If you sign in through your own Google app, turn on the
[Google Meet API](https://console.cloud.google.com/apis/library/meet.googleapis.com) in its project first.

## What your agent tells you

Ask in your own words:

```text prompt
Find my Google Meet calls from the last month about the Harbour budget. Quote what was decided, with the call, its
date and the speaker. Tell me which calls have no transcript yet.
```

To search your calls with your messages and notes, the agent runs `search all`:

```sh
gcal personal search all "budget"
```

`search all` finds a call by its title and by the words in its transcript, as soon as a sync has saved it.

A useful answer names each call, its date and the passage it quotes. An empty answer does not prove that a call
never happened: see [If a call or transcript is missing](#if-a-call-or-transcript-is-missing).

## Find a person across calls

`meetings people` finds the participants of your calls by a name or an email address:

```sh
gcal personal meetings people "Ana Example"
```

When the matches belong to more than one person, `ambiguity.multiple_identities` is `true`. Choose by the email
address: a matching name alone does not prove that it is the same person.

## Save a call as a file

```sh
gcal personal meetings export 12 --format markdown --output harbour-call.md
gcal personal meetings export 12 --format vtt --output harbour-call.vtt
```

Markdown holds the whole meeting. WebVTT holds the transcript only, as subtitles with times. `--output` writes a
new file.

## Add a Meet link or make a room

To give a new event its own Meet link, sign in with `--grant write`; it needs no Meet permission:

```sh
gcal personal events create 3 --title "Sync" --start-time 2026-11-20T10:00 --end-time 2026-11-20T10:30 \
  --conference google-meet
```

An event that already has a link is refused (`event.conference-exists`), so its attendees keep the link they have.
More on changing events is in [the usage guide](./gcal/usage.md#change-events).

A room is the place with one link that calls happen in. After `session start --grant write,meetings`,
`meetings create` makes a room that starts now, and `meetings update` changes a room `gcal` made:

```sh
gcal personal meetings create --setting access_type=trusted --setting auto_transcription=true
gcal personal meetings update spaces/jQCFfuBOdN5z --setting moderation=true
```

`access_type` sets who joins without knocking: `open`, `trusted` or `restricted`. `auto_transcription`,
`auto_recording` and `auto_smart_notes` turn on the transcript, the recording and Gemini's notes in every call of the
room. Both commands ask first; `--yes` answers for a script. A room has no title or time: the calendar event carries
them.

## Which calls you get

- **The calls you organized.** Google lists only those. A call you joined in someone else's room is not listed, but
  `meetings show` opens it by Google's id of the call.
- **The last 30 days.** Google deletes a call's record, its participants and its transcript 30 days after the call.
  A call already in the store stays there, and the transcript Doc stays in Google Drive.
- **Transcripts after the call.** Google makes the transcript after the call ends. Until it is ready, the sync keeps
  the call due and reads it again next time.
- **Transcripts on Google Workspace only.** A personal Google account gets calls and participants, but no
  transcript.

## Keep your calls up to date

Run the sync at least every few weeks, so no call passes Google's 30 days first:

```sh
gcal personal meetings sync
gcal personal meetings sync --since-time 2026-11-01
gcal personal meetings sync --rescan
```

A sync goes on from where the last one stopped. `--since-time` starts from a date, and `--rescan` reads the last 30
days again. When Google limits the requests, the sync keeps what it saved and says when to run it again. When one call
has waited for its transcript for 25 days or more, the sync's answer says since when.

To run it on its own, install a timer as for [feeds](./gcal/usage.md#run-it-on-a-timer):

```sh
gcal personal schedule command meet --every 1d -- meetings sync
```

## What a meeting keeps

- **When the call started and ended, and who joined.**
- **The transcript**, line by line with the speaker.
- **The Drive files Google made**: the transcript Doc, the recording and Gemini's notes, by their Drive file id.
  `gcal` does not download them and asks for no Drive permission. The [Google Drive tool](./drive/index.md) reads
  them.
- **The calendar event.** A call joins its event by the room's link (`meet.google.com/abc-defg-hij`). Meet gives a
  call no title, so the event's title is used.

`meetings list` and `meetings show` ask Google and the store at once, and save what Google returns. Each meeting says
where it was found: `found_in` is `server`, `archive` or `both`. Add `--backend archive` to read the store only,
without the internet.

Participant names and what they said come from other people. `gcal` marks them as untrusted, so your agent reads them
as data and never follows them as instructions.

## If a call or transcript is missing

1. **"this profile signed in without access to Google Meet".** Run the `session start --grant …` the message gives.
   It names the permissions the profile already has, so you keep them.
2. **"the Google Meet API is turned off for this OAuth project".** Your own Google app's project has the Meet API off.
   [Turn it on](https://console.cloud.google.com/apis/library/meet.googleapis.com), wait a few minutes and run
   `meetings sync` again.
3. **"Google Meet has not finished the transcript yet".** Nothing to do: the next sync reads it. `meetings show`
   lists what is still missing under `missing`.
4. **The call is not in the list.** Someone else organized it. Open it with `meetings show` and Google's id of the
   call, or check that it is not older than 30 days.
5. **No transcript at all.** Transcription was off for that call, or the account is a personal Google account.
6. **A word search misses a transcript.** `search all` says under `missing` that the meetings' search index is behind.
   Run `meetings sync` again: a sync stopped by its time limit finishes the index on the next run. Until then, read
   the call with `meetings transcript`.

More sign-in problems are in [gcal troubleshooting](./gcal/troubleshooting.md). Next: [Meetings](./zm/meetings.md)
shows how to connect what was decided on a call to your projects and prepare for the next one.
