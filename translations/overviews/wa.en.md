wa is a command-line tool that lets your AI agent read saved WhatsApp chats, search messages and help you reply. It connects your personal account as a linked device; you can also run its commands yourself. Business-number bots use a separate [Cloud API connection](./bot.md).

## Choose how to start

[Install and link your account](./installation.md) with your agent or by hand. You approve the QR code on your phone; the guide explains what arrives in the local history and how to check the chosen account.

## After connecting

- [Read and search chats](./usage.md), then [check an older agreement](./search.md).
- [Keep and verify files](./attachments.md) and understand [history coverage](./archive.md).
- [Choose agent permissions](./permissions.md) or [connect an MCP app](./mcp.md).
- [Review group members and questions](./groups.md) before choosing an administrative action.

Exact commands are in the [reference](./commands.md); [troubleshooting](./troubleshooting.md) helps with connection failures, and the [roadmap](./roadmap.md) lists future work.
