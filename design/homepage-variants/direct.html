A few useful details

Do I need to switch AI agents?

No. WireCat provides CLI tools, skills and MCP connections for compatible agents. Use the setup guide for your client.

Can I start with my own account?

Yes. Connect your Telegram or MAX account to work with your existing conversations. Bots use separate credentials and their own messaging access.

Does it act automatically?

Your agent uses the tools when instructed. Recurring reviews and monitoring need an agent schedule or service configuration. You can configure permissions and ask before changes.
