Why it works this well

Practical features that help your agent handle the work you normally do in chat apps.

Search stored history offline
Look through the archive on your computer without fetching every message again.
Keep work and personal accounts separate
Use profiles for different accounts and bots. Choose which ones a task should use.
Read voice notes as text
Get a transcript, then ask your agent to pick out the questions and next steps.
Send messages at the right time
Schedule reminders and follow-ups. Telegram delivers them even when your laptop is off.
Search words or conversation meaning
Use exact message filters, or build and embed conversations for semantic retrieval.
Check what was sent
Review send attempts in the journal. Record command runs when you need to inspect a task.
