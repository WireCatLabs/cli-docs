Morning brief paper-workflow

Preserved component

Everything your messenger made you do by hand

Ask in plain words. Your agent runs the command, reads the answer and gets back to you.

  • Tells you who is waiting

    tg review --unanswered

    Every chat where someone asked and you never answered, with how long they have waited.

  • Reads the inbox for you

    tg inbox --new

    Only what arrived since the last look. Muted groups stay quiet unless they mention you.

  • Finds that message from March

    tg messages search

    Your chats live in a copy on your computer. Search years of history, even on a plane.

  • Turns voice notes into text

    tg messages transcribe --local

    By Telegram, or by a speech model on your own machine in 25 languages.

  • Sends later, with the laptop shut

    tg messages send --at 2h

    Telegram itself delivers it at the time you set.

  • Watches while you work

    tg watch

    New messages, edits and reactions as they happen, so your agent can react first.

  • Keeps your MAX groups clean

    max bot chats check

    Spam, stranger links and flood removed by your rules, through the official MAX Bot API.

  • Hands you the whole chat

    tg export --format markdown

    Any conversation as a transcript you or a model can read in one go.
