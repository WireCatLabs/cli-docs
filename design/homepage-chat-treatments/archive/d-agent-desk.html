What your agent runs

Real commands, one job each. Your agent picks them. You can type them too.

tg review --since-time 7d

Who owes what. Everything said this week, yours too, sorted into what you owe and what others owe you.

tg inbox

The morning look. Other people's unread messages in every chat. Nothing gets marked read.

tg review --unanswered 1d

Questions left hanging. Asked at least a day ago, still without an answer.

tg messages send me "Call Mum" --at-time 2026-10-02T18:00

Reminders. Lands in your Saved Messages. Telegram sends it, even with the laptop shut.

tg messages search "paid" --chat "Lisbon trip"

Money and dates. Searches every message kept on your computer, without connecting.

tg messages transcribe "Mum" 8812 --local

Voice notes as text. By Telegram, or by a speech model on your machine.

tg watch

As it happens. New messages arrive in your agent the moment they land.

tg store export "Project Alpha" --format markdown

A whole chat as a document. For a weekly report, or for a model to read in one go.
