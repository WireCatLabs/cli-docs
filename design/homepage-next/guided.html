How do these tools work together?

Your agent coordinates the tools you have configured. WireCat retrieves and acts on messaging data; Himalaya handles email; your agent reads or edits notes in your vault. Setup varies by workflow.

Semantic conversation retrieval currently searches built and embedded conversations within one account. Bringing other sources into an answer is a separate agent workflow.

Where is my information processed?

The messaging archive is stored on your computer. Content you give your agent follows that agent’s local or cloud model configuration. Email and notes keep their own storage and access settings.
