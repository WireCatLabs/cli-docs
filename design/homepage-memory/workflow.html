Connect your messaging,
email and notes

Start with the tools you use. Telegram and MAX are WireCat’s messaging tools. Email through Himalaya and your Obsidian vault can join the same agent workflow.

More messenger connections are planned. You can use today’s tools on their own.

How does this fit together?

Your agent coordinates the tools you connect. WireCat supplies the messaging tools; Himalaya is a separate open-source email tool; Obsidian is your existing vault.

For example, ask your agent to collect a discussion, check the latest email and prepare an update to your project note.

Why it works this well

Practical features that help your agent handle the work you normally do in chat apps.

Search stored history offline
Look through the archive on your computer without fetching every message again.
Keep work and personal accounts separate
Use profiles for different accounts and bots. Choose which ones a task should use.
Read voice notes as text
Get a transcript, then ask your agent to pick out the questions and next steps.
Send messages at the right time
Schedule reminders and follow-ups. Telegram delivers them even when your laptop is off.
Search words or conversation meaning
Use exact message filters, or build and embed conversations for semantic retrieval.
Check what was sent
Review send attempts in the journal. Record command runs when you need to inspect a task.

Where is my information processed?

The messaging archive is stored on your computer. Content given to your agent follows its local or cloud model configuration. Email and notes keep their own storage and access settings.
