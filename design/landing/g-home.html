A day with your agent

You ask in plain words. Your agent runs one tg command and gets back to you with the answer, not the chat.

The morning summary

tg inbox --new

Set it once as a schedule. Before you open the app, your agent has read everything that came in overnight and written you one message.

Muted groups stay out of it unless someone mentions you. Nothing is marked as read, so nobody sees you looked.

Your bots, through the official Bot API of Telegram and MAX. Keep as many as you like, each under a name you choose.

The bot's name is the first word.

Keep several, each under a name you choose.

tg bot list --check

Tokens in the system keyring.

Never printed, not even in an error.

tg sales bot auth set

Each bot has its own list of chats.

It writes only there, and keeps a journal of what it sent.

tg sales bot recipients add user:<id>

Messages and files.

Your own account, as one more of your devices: every chat, its history, groups, channels and contacts.

  1. Unread in every chat at once. Other people's messages, and nothing gets marked read.
  2. Who owes what. Everything said since the last review, yours too, in one call.
  3. Search years of history offline. In the copy of your chats kept on your computer.
  4. Voice notes as text. By Telegram, or by a speech model on your machine.
  5. Send later. Telegram delivers it on time, with your laptop shut.
  6. Everything else you do by hand. Replies, files, reactions, polls, edits, forwards, pins.

Why it works this well

Built for agents and scripts first, so it is quick, predictable and leaves a trail.

Fast
Search runs on the copy of your chats on your computer. No network, no waiting.
tg messages search "contract" --offline
Always current
A background service keeps that copy up to date, and catches up after a restart.
tg server install
Many accounts, many bots
Each profile and each bot has a name, and the name is the first word of the command.
tg work inbox
Reads without a trace
Reading marks nothing read. The other side sees nothing until you choose.
tg chats mark-read
Never sends twice
If the connection drops mid-send, repeat the command. Telegram recognises it and drops the copy.
--send-id
Logs, not your messages
Every send goes into a journal and every call can be traced, without text, names or numbers.
tg sends list · --trace
Secrets stay in the keyring
No password, login code, phone number or token is ever typed on the command line.
tg session start
Made for agents
One JSON value on stdout, errors on stderr, a fixed exit code for every kind of failure.
--json
Runs everywhere
Windows, macOS and Linux, on Node 22 or newer. No native module to build.
npm i -g @leemour/tg-cli
