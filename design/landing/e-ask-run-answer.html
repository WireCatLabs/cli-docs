The commands behind it

Grouped by the job you want done. With --json, or when piped, each one prints a single JSON value and nothing else.

Catch up

What came in, without marking anything read.

  • tg inboxunread in every chat
  • tg inbox --newonly what arrived since the last check
  • tg watchnew messages as they arrive

Who owes what

Promises and questions, yours and theirs.

  • tg review --since-time 7da week of messages, yours too
  • tg review --unanswered 1dquestions still open after a day
  • tg review --chat "Hiking" --unansweredin a group you run

Remember

Telegram sends it later, even with this computer off.

  • tg messages send me "Call Mum" --at-time 2ha reminder in Saved Messages
  • tg messages send "Team" "Minutes" --file minutes.pdf --at-time 2026-10-05T09:00on Monday at nine

Find

Searches the copy on your computer. No connection needed.

  • tg search messages "contract"every word, across all chats
  • tg search messages "paid" --chat "Lisbon trip"in one chat
  • tg store export "Project Alpha" --format markdowna whole chat as a document

Listen

Voice notes as text, by Telegram or on your machine.

  • tg messages transcribe "Mum" 8812 --localone voice note
  • tg inbox --transcribeevery new voice note, as text

Groups you run

Who came, who left, who is still waiting for an answer.

  • tg chats events "Hiking" --since-time 7djoins, leaves, adds, removals
  • tg chats members list "Hiking" --alleveryone, with their role
  • max chats check "Residents"on MAX: spam and flood, by your rules
