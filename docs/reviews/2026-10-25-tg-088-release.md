# Telegram 0.88.0 reviewed publication

Readers can save a reply as a Telegram draft to check and send from the app, see reaction changes in
`watch --events` and `serve`, and find every command in discovery with its current availability and the
permission that blocks it. The portal targets published tg 0.88.0 with Core 0.408.0.
Reviewer: Claude.

## Sources and boundaries

- [Published release](https://github.com/WireCatLabs/tg-cli/releases/tag/v0.88.0) and
  [successful validation/publication](https://github.com/WireCatLabs/tg-cli/actions/runs/40134038271).
- Exact npm discovery was captured with an isolated home and blocked network/keyring sockets.
- Runtime errata were checked against the installed 0.88.0 package, not inferred from prose:
  `toolsFromCommands` in Core 0.408 drops every command with a `lifetime` (`setup`, `watch`, `serve`,
  `bot watch`); the contract has no `chats delete` or `chats clear` command; `messages press`,
  `chats start` and `chats app` are present; `--operation-id` exists on `messages send`,
  `messages forward` and `polls create`; `upgrade` is authority-gated (`upgrade --check` is not).
