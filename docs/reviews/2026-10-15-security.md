## Behavior sources

- MAX v0.54.0 pins cli-core 0.285.0; Telegram v0.60.0 pins 0.288.0. These are also the
  reviewed runtime releases in `tools.json`; this change does not advance their pins.
- [cli-core v0.285.0 schedules](https://github.com/WireCatLabs/cli-core/blob/v0.285.0/src/background/schedules.ts)
  sets the read-only environment lock for scheduled agents. Its authority-granting command
  metadata rejects settings/permissions/recipient/schedule changes under that lock. MAX's
  native coverage is [PR652](https://github.com/WireCatLabs/max-cli/pull/652), included in v0.54.0.
  The guide explicitly limits this promise to operations through tg/max; other executable
  programs are outside the lock. Preparing reply text does not imply saving an app draft.
- [cli-core v0.285.0 upload guard](https://github.com/WireCatLabs/cli-core/blob/v0.285.0/src/sends/upload.ts)
  checks canonical and original paths against credential/state locations while permitting
  ordinary hidden folders; `--allow-any-file` is the explicit exception. Native MAX and TG
  paths are covered by [MAX637](https://github.com/WireCatLabs/max-cli/pull/637) and
  [TG507](https://github.com/WireCatLabs/tg-cli/pull/507).
- The shared [release checks at v0.31.0](https://github.com/WireCatLabs/cli-base/blob/v0.31.0/.github/workflows/release-checks.yml)
  include production audit at critical severity, secret/history checks, workflow analysis and
  CodeQL security severity >=9. Scanner failures and secret findings also fail their gates;
  noncritical advisory/source findings remain for review. The release and deployment jobs
  depend on these checks. Packages use trusted publishing with provenance.
- The organization security configuration enables private reporting for public tool
  repositories. Reporting links still lead to the owning repository; no vulnerability details
  or account data are included here.
